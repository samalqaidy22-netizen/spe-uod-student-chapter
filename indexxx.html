About Us

The SPE Student Chapter at the University of Duhok is part of the Society of Petroleum Engineers. We connect students with industry knowledge, workshops, and global engineering opportunities.

SPE is the world’s largest non-profit professional organization serving the upstream energy sector, with more than 132,000 members across 146 countries, working together to advance technical knowledge, innovation, and professional development.

To empower students and future professionals by providing technical knowledge, practical experience, and leadership opportunities in the petroleum and energy fields.

  • Build your skills and confidence
  • Gain real-world experience
  • Expand your professional network
  • Enhance your CV
  • Be part of a global community

  • Field trips & industry visits
  • Networking with professionals
